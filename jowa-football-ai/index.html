Jowa Football AI

AI-Powered Football Game — 3v3, 6v6 & 11v11 · Open Source

Open Source Python Reinforcement Learning v3 · 11v11

About

Jowa Football AI is an open-source football simulation where the players are controlled by AI that learns by playing. It started as 3v3 with a Q-table (v1), grew to 6v6 with a DQN (v2), and v3 is a full 11v11 match between two brains trained with MAPPO and an entity transformer.
