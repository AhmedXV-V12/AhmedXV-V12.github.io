About the Game

Jowa Football AI is an open-source football simulation game featuring intelligent AI players that learn and adapt using Q-table reinforcement learning. Experience dynamic 3v3 matches where AI opponents continuously improve their gameplay.

🤖 Advanced AI Learning

AI players learn and improve using Q-table reinforcement learning algorithms

🎮 Multiple Play Modes

Play solo against AI or team up with friends in 1, 2, or 3 player modes

⚽ 3v3 Football Action

Fast-paced 3v3 matches with intelligent team coordination

💻 Cross-Platform

Available for both Windows and Linux operating systems

Game Controls

Player 1

↑ Up  |  ↓ Down  |  ← Left  |  → Right

Player 2

U Up  |  J Down  |  G Left  |  K Right

Player 3

W Up  |  S Down  |  A Left  |  D Right
